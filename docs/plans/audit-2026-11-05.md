## The owner's triage, 2026-11-06

Decisions taken at triage, recorded so the steps below are not re-argued:

- **FT2 — fix shape:** the compose build context defaults to a path that
  cannot exist by accident (`${SELFPOST_SOURCE:-./source}`), failing at once
  and naming itself. The override-file alternative was declined.
- **E1 — placed.** The absent absolute session cap goes under *Accepted
  risks* in [security.md](../security.md) (the history paragraph's "left to
  the owner" note closes with it).
- **M1 — scope:** the 72-byte ceiling is added to the administrator password
  *and* the backup / export password forms are made to read the same way
  (one rule, one wording; the floor stays counted in characters, the ceiling
  in bytes).
- **FT6 — both halves:** `postqueueTimeout` becomes a var for the test, and
  the product stops discarding a truncation it already knew about when the
  deadline fires.
- **The declinable nits — all three taken:** FT7, FT8 and FT10 are fixed,
  not just recorded.
- **The test suite's cost — all five recommendations**, in the measured
  order of gain for risk (bcrypt first).
- **Coverage — declined.** No second line-by-line pass over
  `internal/milter/{sign,sender,milter}.go` + `build/postfix-config.sh` or
  over `internal/web/view`: both reviews found nothing there, and the
  2026-10 audits covered those zones.
- **No refactoring**, per the audit's § *Refactoring and optimisation*: the
  layering, the seams and the citation discipline hold; splitting files,
  an ORM, a front-end build step and a Go rewrite of `postfix-config.sh`
  stay declined. The one structural decision — `SetMaxOpenConns(1)` — keeps
  its revisit trigger as written there (milter queries waiting toward the
  Postfix timeouts; then a second read-only connection, *after* the queue
  is shortened, not before).

The findings were spot-checked against the tree at `08730ff` on 2026-11-06
before this plan was written: M1, M2, FT1, FT2, FT3, FT5/L2, FT6, FT7, L1,
L3, L4, L5, N1 and E1 all read as the reviews describe them. The one place
the two code reviews differed — FT6's product consequence — was settled by
reading `internal/postfix/queue.go`: the truncation is known before the
deadline check and is returned as `false`, so the audit's reading stands.

  before tagging; the tag reaches GitHub through the mirror.

  tests, and a removed store method once cost a round trip to the e2e stand
