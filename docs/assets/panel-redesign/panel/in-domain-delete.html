Delete lists.example.org

This cannot be undone

Deleting the inbound domain lists.example.org will:

  • stop accepting mail for it on port 25 — senders get a relay-denied reply;
  • remove its upstream and its 4 listed recipients.

Remove or repoint the MX record first, or mail for the domain will bounce. Outbound domains are not touched.
